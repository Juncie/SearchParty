“AI fills in gaps” needs an important constraint: it must never invent personal facts.

Divide profile information into three categories:

Facts: name, work history, education, authorization, skills.
Preferences: salary, target roles, location, work environment.
Narratives: “What do you bring to a team?”, cover letters, motivation answers.
AI can safely:

Extract proposed facts from a résumé.
Summarize facts already provided.
Draft narrative answers from verified evidence.
Ask the user focused follow-up questions.
Recommend a salary range, clearly labeled as a recommendation.
It should not silently decide authorization, salary expectations, demographic answers, credentials, or employment history.
